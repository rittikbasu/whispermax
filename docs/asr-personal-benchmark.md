# Personal ASR benchmark

YapLab is the separate local project for comparing WhisperMax's model with
alternatives on the owner's recordings. Its README and `BENCHMARK.md` describe
the current corpus, scoring, recipes, and decision gates.

The earlier v2 recorder and scoring workflow in this repository has been
superseded. Historical results and their limitations remain in
`phonon-asr-evaluation.md`; they are not a substitute for the current benchmark.

Keep audio, references, predictions, and private reports out of Git. Synthetic
speech is not part of the personal ASR accuracy benchmark.
